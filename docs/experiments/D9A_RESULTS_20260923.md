D9A measures `CE(R01,y) - CE(R05,y)` on a fixed uniform sample of up to
200,000 valid NoBoundary pixels per tile. Positive values mean that R05 lowers
cross-entropy. Ranking excludes pixels with absolute benefit <=0.01 CE in the
primary row. AUROC/AUPRC use sampled, spatially correlated pixels and are
descriptive Val2 metrics, not independent-sample significance tests. Full-tile
mIoU uses all valid pixels. ECE is absent because these direct scores are not
calibrated benefit probabilities.

## Main finding

The direct confidence difference outperforms the tested
`confidence_difference * Jensen-Shannon_disagreement` score on benefit AUROC
and AUPRC in **all 16** seed-by-condition comparisons. Across the rounded
summary, its AUROC advantage is 0.0048–0.0194 (mean 0.0141) and AUPRC
advantage is 0.0092–0.0537 (mean 0.0211). This is evidence against that
specific naive disagreement interaction as an improvement over confidence.
It does not test learned combinations, semantic prototypes or frequency
quality; therefore the full D9 gate is **not evaluated**.

## Decision and remaining audit

Do not train a safety residual gate from D9A. The specific confidence×JS
interaction is closed as a proposed improvement. Before deciding whether a
more meaningful benefit predictor is justified, inspect the server's
`strata_table.csv` for Building, Car, small Car and locally damaged regions,
plus the entropy and class-agreement score columns in `summary_table.csv`.
These checks are exploratory and cannot select a fitted model on repeatedly
inspected Val2. Any fitted semantic/frequency combination and ECE analysis
requires a new, frozen, spatially independent source. Historical TEST6 remains
unavailable for this selection.
