D9A measures `CE(R01,y) - CE(R05,y)` on a fixed uniform sample of up to
200,000 valid NoBoundary pixels per tile. Positive values mean that R05 lowers
cross-entropy. Ranking excludes pixels with absolute benefit <=0.01 CE in the
primary row. AUROC/AUPRC use sampled, spatially correlated pixels and are
descriptive Val2 metrics, not independent-sample significance tests. Full-tile
mIoU uses all valid pixels. ECE is absent because these direct scores are not
calibrated benefit probabilities.

## Main finding

The direct confidence difference outperforms the tested
`confidence_difference * Jensen-Shannon_disagreement` score on benefit AUROC
and AUPRC in **all 16** seed-by-condition comparisons. Across the rounded
summary, its AUROC advantage is 0.0048–0.0194 (mean 0.0141) and AUPRC
advantage is 0.0092–0.0537 (mean 0.0211). This is evidence against that
specific naive disagreement interaction as an improvement over confidence.
It does not test learned combinations, semantic prototypes or frequency
quality; therefore the full D9 gate is **not evaluated**.

A subsequent read-only review of the same D9A files finds that the **entropy
difference beats confidence difference in all 16 comparisons** for both
benefit AUROC and AUPRC. Its mean advantages are +0.0219 AUROC and +0.0258
AUPRC; the minimum non-neutral sample support is 154,827 pixels. This makes
entropy the strongest observed direct ranking baseline for the next comparison.
Entropy-based routing is already a known control, and better CE-benefit
ranking does not establish better mIoU or lower selected harm. Existing D5
`min_entropy` results must be checked before treating the ranking gain as a
safe decision rule.

The second read-only review confirms the entropy advantage across relevant
strata. For Car it wins both AUROC and AUPRC in 16/16 comparisons (mean
+0.0160/+0.0380); for small Car it wins 16/16 AUROC and 15/16 AUPRC
(mean +0.0291/+0.0335). It wins both metrics in 4/4 locally damaged-region
comparisons (mean +0.0273/+0.0237). Building is less uniform: 15/16 AUROC
and 14/16 AUPRC wins. The smallest small-Car stratum has only 158 sampled
pixels, and these repeated Val2 strata cannot establish external validity.

interaction is closed as a proposed improvement. It also loses mean AUROC
and AUPRC to confidence in Building, Car, small Car and locally damaged
strata; the smallest small-Car stratum has only 158 sampled pixels.
