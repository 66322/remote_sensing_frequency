## Primary result

On expert-disagreement pixels, the target was whether R05 alone or R01 alone
predicted the true class. A stronger direct control was chosen separately for
AUROC and AUPRC from confidence and entropy. The six scores were fixed before
the run and were not fitted or calibrated on Val2.

| RGB–DSM edge alignment | 0 | 0 | -0.1021 | -0.0950 |

The single combination AUROC win was seed17 local DSM-zero quadrant:
0.6255 versus confidence 0.6201, while its AUPRC was 0.5048 versus
confidence 0.5214. It does not meet the cross-seed or joint-metric gate.
The semantic/frequency controls are handcrafted input-space proxies, not
faithful replications of SGMA or CAF-Net.

Confidence difference was the best AUROC control in all 16 pooled rows.
Entropy difference had slightly higher AUPRC in 6 seed42 conditions but
lost all eight seed17 AUPRC comparisons. This differs from D9A's CE-benefit
ranking result: a higher true-class probability does not always change the
segmentation decision in the right direction.

Severe DSM noise illustrates the target's importance. In 400,000 sampled
NoBoundary pixels per seed, R05-only-correct versus R01-only-correct counts
were 15,106 versus 33,419 at seed42 and 13,158 versus 43,474 at seed17.
Thus there are many more decision-changing harms than gains under this
condition. These counts do not themselves establish a routing improvement.

## Decision and limits

**Do not train the proposed safety residual gate from these D9B features.**
The combination fails the prespecified requirement to consistently exceed
confidence, entropy and semantic-only controls across both seeds. D9B closes
this exact unfitted six-score screen on Val2. It does not rule out learned
deep-feature semantic prototypes, better image-quality cues or other
benefit-aware approaches, and it cannot assess independent generalization.

The next substantive step is to freeze a genuinely independent labelled
RGB–DSM segmentation source, with spatially disjoint fit/calibration/evaluation
scenes and compatible class mapping. Only then can a new learned benefit
predictor be compared with confidence, entropy and stronger semantic/frequency
baselines on actual negative transfer, class IoU and severe degradations.
If such a source is unavailable, retain the negative D9A/D9B evidence and
avoid a deployment or new-method claim. Historical Test6 remains frozen.
