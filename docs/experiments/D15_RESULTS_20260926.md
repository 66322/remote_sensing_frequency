## Mechanistic interpretation and limits

- DSM-only is substantially weaker overall than RGB, but Building IoU is 90.847/90.538%, versus R01 88.389/88.422% on clean. Its value is class-specific; it does not replace the RGB expert.
- RGB down4 is the strongest supported target. R05 exceeds the better RGB control by +2.9585/+1.9245 pp pooled. The per-tile gains are +2.3922/+3.6903 pp for seed42 and +0.4278/+3.5171 pp for seed17. All four tile/seed comparisons are positive.
- Mild degradations are less stable by tile. Seed17 has negative gains versus the stronger RGB control on tile `3_11` for down2 (-0.8083pp), blur (-0.5793pp), and local down4 (-0.5149pp). Therefore do not generalize the pooled gate into a universal robustness claim.
- On RGB down4, R05 versus C-OO-QD improves Building IoU by +7.547/+2.765 pp and Tree by +3.828/+5.139 pp. Car IoU is lower by approximately -0.814/-0.198 pp. Focus the next structure-recovery hypothesis on Building/Tree, not an unsupported Car benefit.
- Under down4, setting DSM to zero decreases R05 mIoU by 6.0086/5.7477 pp. The R01 and C-OO-QD matrices remain identical to their RGB-down4 results. This supports a DSM-specific contribution in this condition, although zeroing is an intervention and distribution shift, not a calibrated availability/fallback guarantee.
- R01+DSM-only ground-truth-informed oracle mIoU is 88.2922/89.1584% under down4. This diagnoses fixed-expert complementarity; it is neither deployable nor a global architecture upper bound.

## Next decision

The research problem remains RGB-DSM complementarity and reliable segmentation. RGB down4 provides a useful stress condition; it does not establish a new low-resolution/SR research direction or prove that feature restoration is the necessary mechanism. The initial post-D15 proposal to prioritize low-resolution recovery was premature and is superseded by `docs/protocols/D16_D17_RESEARCH_PORTFOLIO_20260926.md`: a bounded paired-intervention learning batch and an independent geometry-supervision alternative. New methods must beat matched ordinary-fusion/regularization controls, not only R01. Clean and both RGB/DSM perturbation families remain in the evaluation; old D15 frozen results do not substitute for newly changed training controls.

Do not add frequency modules in the first method comparison. If a complementary-learning mechanism passes both-seed and per-tile checks, test FreqFusion and any independent modification as later ablations. The existing two-tile Val2 remains exploratory; an independent scene source and subsequent replication are required for thesis confirmation. Next method code/queue is not yet implemented or launched in this handoff.
