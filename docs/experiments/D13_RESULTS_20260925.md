## Paired source-file diagnosis (2026-09-26)

All eight transferred summaries identify commit `afea788`, the configured
4000 training steps, the same seed-matched R01 anchor and initial head within
each seed, and identical treatment counts. Their config SHA-256 matches the
Git blob; all report `test6_loaded=false`. For every arm and condition,
pooled 6×6 confusion matrices equal the sums of the two Val2 tile matrices;
class support and harm/rescue counts reconcile with the recorded totals.

Thus the DSM-specific increment beyond matched RGB capacity is small and
reverses sign across seeds under both losses. The risk loss reduces both H
and R in the RGB-only as well as DSM arms. For seed 42 it lowers family mIoU
relative to each CE counterpart; for seed 17 it raises mIoU relative to CE,
but DSM-risk remains below RGB-risk and clean frozen R01. This pattern
supports a broad suppression of corrections, not a demonstrated selective
retention of beneficial DSM corrections.

Across the six available-DSM evaluation conditions, the within-arm spread
of pooled NB mIoU is at most 0.0093 pp (DSM-CE seed 42); other arms have
smaller spreads. The present condition matrix therefore does not
demonstrate meaningful response to these DSM degradations. It also does
**not** prove the head ignores DSM: a controlled input intervention with
availability held fixed would be needed for that causal claim.

Clean per-class IoU shows a mixed trade-off rather than a stable class gain.
For seed 42, DSM-CE minus RGB-CE is +0.27 pp on Building but -0.14 pp on
Tree; for seed 17, it is +0.06 pp on Building but -0.21 pp on Tree and
-0.10 pp on Car (rounded from source values). In seed 17's clean
DSM-CE arm, Tree harm is 1.127% of Tree pixels, versus 0.728% for RGB-CE;
DSM-risk lowers Tree harm to 0.186%, while Tree rescue falls to 0.013%,
versus 0.077% for DSM-CE. These are true-class rates on the two Val2 tiles,
not scene-level estimates. The saved aggregate counts do not identify
which individual pixels overlap between arms.

**Decision.** Close this exact D13 Stage 1 design and do not run its conditional
semantic/frequency Stage 2, repeat the same Val2 screen, or loosen the gate
after observing the result. Before designing another training objective,
base the next design on the small, seed-unstable DSM increment and the
suppression of both harm and rescue. A bounded inference-only intervention
that changes DSM while holding availability fixed could check whether the
trained DSM heads use the modality. It requires a separate frozen protocol
and would remain exploratory on Val2; do not launch more training from this
gate. The transferred `summary.json` files are original server evaluation
outputs; training logs and checkpoints remain on the server. Val2 has been
repeatedly inspected and supports exploratory development only; Test6 stays
frozen. No independent generalization or statistical safety claim follows.
