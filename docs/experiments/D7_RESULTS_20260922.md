# D7 data, scale and semantic-error audit

D7 completed all stages on commit `b3b31e6` with zero failures and without loading
historical TEST6. Train16 has a 15.9175x foreground max/min pixel ratio. Car is
1.7561% of label pixels, 1.7671% of simulated crop pixels and absent from 51.585%
of individual random crops. With effective batch 16, this does not establish
optimizer-step starvation.

Small-Car correctness is lower than medium/large Car across both seeds and both
Val2 tiles. The largest recall deficit occurs on tile `5_10`. Car is not the worst
semantic class: R01 FullGT Car IoU is 82.1396/82.2260 for seeds 42/17 while Tree
is 74.1757/74.7491. R05 improves Building by 6.9432/3.7988 pp over R01 but lowers
Car by 2.3276/1.6972 pp. DSM down8 does not show replicated Car-specific excess
vulnerability. The evidence supports a small-object sampling screen and
class-dependent fusion analysis, not a generic long-tail or weighted-loss claim.

D8 therefore separates random crops, any-Car-centred crops and small-Car-centred
crops on frozen R01 seed42 initialization. Weighted CE, compound losses and
boundary supervision remain deferred.
